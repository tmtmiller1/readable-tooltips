==== 1.0.5 | 2026-09-21 | src 4e7ad0431367 | gen 7aa1d3e46dbc ====
[b]Version 1.0.5 · 21 September 2026[/b]
Updated for Civilization VII 1.5.0. Every tooltip that follows the cursor now sits clear of it, including the new map-tile tooltip that 1.5.0 introduced.

[b]Added[/b]
• [b]Map-tile tooltips.[/b] 1.5.0 replaced the tooltip you see when hovering over a map tile, and the new one sits close to the cursor. The mod now moves it 12px further away. This also works when another mod replaces the map-tile tooltip, such as QD Improved Plot Tooltip.
• [b]Relationship, trade-route, peace-deal and production tooltips.[/b] These get the same extra 12px. Near a screen edge, where the game flips them to the other side of the cursor, they used to sit only 5-6px from it; they now sit 17-18px away.

[b]Fixed[/b]
• [b]Tooltips near the right or bottom edge of the screen.[/b] When the game flipped a tooltip to the left of or above the cursor, the mod still pushed it down and to the right, back toward the cursor. In the bottom-right corner it covered the cursor. The extra space is now added on the side the tooltip actually opens.

==== 1.0.4 | 2026-07-18 | src 5ae82ec989e9 | gen 06d665a7ae18 ====
[b]Version 1.0.4 · 18 July 2026[/b]
The offset now works in game, and it no longer cuts off the tech and civic “what you unlocked” popup.

[b]Fixed[/b]
• [b]The mod now works in saved games.[/b] It was missing a setting that marks it as safe for existing saves, so the game skipped it whenever you loaded a save.
• [b]Tech and civic “what you unlocked” tooltips no longer get cut off.[/b] The old offset was applied after the game had already fitted the tooltip on screen, which pushed the tall unlock popup past the edge.

[b]Changed[/b]
• [b]Tooltips are moved before the game fits them on screen.[/b] The game then flips and fits the moved tooltip itself, so every tooltip clears the cursor and stays fully on screen in every corner.
• [b]Two spacing levels.[/b] Tooltips over the map and in popups get the full gap. Tooltips on the tech, civic, wonders and legacies buttons get a smaller gap, so they stay close to the button they describe.

[b]Version 1.0.3 · 8 July 2026[/b]

[b]Fixed[/b]
• [b]The “what you unlocked” popup no longer has its top-left corner cut off.[/b] When a tech or civic finished, hovering a newly unlocked item showed a tooltip with its corner clipped. ==== 1.0.0 | 2026-07-04 | src 49cba31a2826 | gen ad606b3d8ec0 ====
[b]Version 1.0.0 · 4 July 2026[/b]
First release. Every tooltip now sits clear of the cursor.

[b]Added[/b]
• [b]Offset every tooltip off the cursor.[/b] The base game's main tooltip system pins a tooltip's top-left corner directly at the cursor, so the first line of text hides under the pointer. Readable Tool Tips translates whatever tooltip is active a small, consistent distance away from the cursor so its text is always clear and readable. Pure position change — no font, size, color, or content is altered.
• [b]Flip-aware offset.[/b] When a tooltip flips to the left of or above the cursor near a screen edge, the offset direction flips with it, so the tooltip is always pushed away from the cursor and never back under it.
• [b]Coexists with other tooltip mods.[/b] The offset is applied to the game's shared tooltip slot without reading or overriding any tooltip's own styling, and with no !important rules, so modded tooltips render unchanged; only their position shifts.
• [b]Single tuning knob.[/b] One OFFSET value in ui/readable-tooltips.js controls how far tooltips sit from the cursor.
